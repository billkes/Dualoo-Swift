# Dualoo Privacy Agreement

Latest Updated: May 18, 2026

## Overview

Dualoo ("the App", "we", "our") is an offline-first antonym photo drill application operated as a single-player, on-device experience. This Privacy Agreement explains what information the App handles, how that information stays under your control, and the limited circumstances in which anything leaves your device. By installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this document in full.

The guiding principle of Dualoo is minimal data handling. The App works without an account, without cloud synchronization, and without advertising or analytics software development kits. Your photographs, scores, captions, coin balances, and drill history are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Who the App Is Intended For

Dualoo is rated **18+** and is intended for users who are **at least 18 years** of age. We do not knowingly direct the App or its marketing toward children, and the App's features, including camera drills and in-app purchases, are designed for an adult audience practicing vocabulary, composition, and mindfulness exercises.

## Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprint for tracking purposes. There is no registration form, no login screen, and no social profile anywhere in the App. Because there is no account system, there is also no password, nickname, avatar, or other account attribute for us to hold.

The App does not run crash-analytics beacons, behavioral funnels, or session-recording scripts. We do not build user profiles, we do not sell data, and we do not share data with advertisers, because no such data ever reaches our side.

## Information Processed On Your Device

The App processes the following categories of information locally on your device, solely to provide its features. Drill content includes the antonym card you drew, the two frames you captured, rail ratings, contrast scores, grades, captions, and vault states. Preference data includes your legal acceptance timestamp, daily drill slot preference, and streak statistics. Wallet data includes your coin balance, free quota usage, and a local transaction ledger. All of this information is persisted in the App's private storage area and is removed when the App is deleted.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

Dualoo requests camera access so you can shoot the left and right frames of each duel, and photo library access so you can import existing frames or save exported strips. Captured frames and exported strips are written only into the app's sandboxed Documents area, and exported strips are copied into your system Photos library only when you explicitly tap the save control. Frames never leave your device through our code, and the App contains no feature that uploads your images.

Camera and photo permissions are requested at the moment a feature needs them, and the App continues offering its scoring, vaulting, and library features if you decline, limited of course by the corresponding capture or save capability. You manage these permissions in your system settings at any time.

## Purchases and Transaction Identifiers

When you buy a coin pack, the purchase is executed by the platform's storefront, not by us. The only identifying value the App handles in that flow is a **Device Unique Identifier**-class transaction token issued by the storefront, which the App records locally to confirm exactly-once coin crediting and to resolve purchase retries. This token is used solely for local purchase bookkeeping, is never correlated with any personal information, and is never sent to any server operated by us.

Free quota counting (daily free draws and the daily free strip export) is computed locally by comparing usage timestamps against your device's calendar day. Nothing about this accounting leaves the device.

## Data Storage and Retention

All App data resides in the sandboxed storage of the application on your device. Drill sessions, vault entries, ring-day ledgers, wallet ledgers, and settings are retained until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App, at which point the operating system removes them entirely along with the sandbox. Because we hold no copy of your data, we are unable to restore it after deletion, which is the intended privacy guarantee rather than a limitation.

## Data Security

We protect your information through technical and organizational measures proportionate to an offline tool. Technically, all media and records are confined to the operating system's per-app sandbox, and media is served to the App's own interface through an isolated local scheme that refuses path traversal and never exposes absolute file paths. Access to frames from outside the App is governed by the operating system's permission model.

Organizationally, the App is engineered to avoid collecting data in the first place, which removes entire categories of breach risk. There is no remote account database to compromise, no backend message queue carrying your content, and no third-party analytics endpoint receiving your behavior.

## Content Safety, Filtering, and Reporting

Although drills are private by design, captions you attach to vaulted pairs are filtered by in-app **filtering methods** that block slurs and abusive terms before text is stored locally. If you encounter any inappropriate content associated with the App, you can reach us through our **user reporting mechanism** by email as described in the Contact Us section; we review every report and take action within **24 hours**, which includes removing offending material from our distribution channels and applying our **zero tolerance** stance toward abusive or unlawful content.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 18, and its age rating is **18+**. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose any information from children, and because the App operates without accounts or network transmission of user content, no personal information from any child is received or processed by us. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that conflicts with this commitment, contact us and we will respond promptly within **24 hours** and take appropriate action, which for this offline architecture means guidance on removing the App and all of its local data from the device.

## International Use

The App is built to be neutral across jurisdictions and does not reference or depend on the specific privacy statutes of any single country or region. The offline, on-device architecture provides substantially the same privacy posture everywhere the App is used. Where local law grants you rights over personal data held by a controller, please remember that all data about your usage exists only on your device and is fully under your own control there.
