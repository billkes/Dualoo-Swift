## Services We Provide

Dualoo is an offline-first photo drill tool. It draws antonym prompt pairs from a seeded local deck, lets you capture two opposite frames through your device's camera or photo library, lets you rate the contrast between them on dual rails, grades the duel, vaults winning pairs into a local library, and renders a Before–After strip that you can save into your system Photos album. The drill rhythm is organized through an on-device seven-day reminder ring, and all of these features function without a network connection.

Certain premium actions in the App spend coins that you purchase as consumable in-app items, including draws beyond the daily free allowance and strip saves beyond the daily free save. Coins have no cash value, cannot be transferred between users, and exist only inside the App on your device.
